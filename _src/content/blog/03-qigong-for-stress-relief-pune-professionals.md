Pune runs on deadlines. Between long hours at IT parks in Hinjewadi, Kharadi and Magarpatta, late-night calls with clients abroad, and traffic on the way home, stress can become a daily companion.

Qigong offers a simple, practical way to bring your nervous system back to calm. You do not need a mat, a special room or even a change of clothes. Here are five practices you can do at your desk, on a break or at home.

## Why qigong helps with stress

When you are stressed, your breathing becomes short and high in the chest, your shoulders lift and your mind jumps from task to task. Qigong gently reverses this: slow movement, deep belly breathing and calm focus signal to your body that it is safe to relax. Health institutions such as the Cleveland Clinic describe qigong as a helpful complement for stress and wellbeing.

## 1. Belly breathing (2 minutes)

Sit or stand tall. Place both hands on your lower belly, just below the navel.

- Breathe in slowly through your nose and let your belly expand into your hands.
- Breathe out gently and let the belly soften.
- Keep the breath smooth and quiet, with no strain.

Ten slow breaths are enough to shift how you feel.

## 2. Lifting the sky (2 minutes)

Stand with feet hip-width apart. Interlace your fingers in front of your belly.

- As you breathe in, slowly raise your hands up in front of you and overhead, palms facing the sky.
- As you breathe out, separate your hands and float them down by your sides.

Repeat 6 to 8 times. This opens the chest and stretches the whole body after sitting.

## 3. Shaking it out (1 minute)

Stand with knees slightly bent. Let your whole body gently bounce up and down from the knees, with arms and shoulders loose. This releases physical tension quickly and is great after a difficult meeting.

## 4. Shoulder rolls with breath (1 minute)

Breathe in as you lift your shoulders toward your ears, then breathe out as you roll them back and down. Repeat 8 times. This is a simple fix for the "laptop hunch".

## 5. Standing stillness (3 minutes)

Stand with knees soft, arms relaxed, as if gently holding a large ball in front of your chest. Breathe naturally and feel your feet on the ground. When thoughts come, return your attention to your breath. This short standing meditation is one of the core practices of qigong.

## Building it into your workday

- Do belly breathing before an important call.
- Use "lifting the sky" every time you get up from your desk.
- Try the full five-practice sequence after you log off, before you start your evening.

Consistency matters more than duration. Ten minutes a day will do more than an hour once a month.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I do qigong in the office?

Yes. Most of these practices can be done standing beside your desk or seated in your chair.

### How quickly will I feel less stressed?

Many people feel calmer within a few minutes of slow breathing. Lasting change comes with daily practice over a few weeks.

### Is qigong a treatment for anxiety disorders?

No. Qigong can support your wellbeing, but anxiety disorders should be assessed and treated by a qualified professional.
