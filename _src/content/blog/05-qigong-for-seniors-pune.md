As we grow older, staying active becomes more important, but many forms of exercise start to feel too harsh on the knees, back and joints. Getting down on the floor for yoga may be difficult, and gyms can feel intimidating.

Qigong is one of the gentlest and most suitable practices for senior citizens. It is slow, can be done standing or seated, and focuses on balance, breathing and calm. Many families in Pune are now introducing their parents and grandparents to qigong as a way to stay mobile and independent.

## Why balance matters

Falls are one of the biggest risks for older adults. A fall can lead to fractures, a loss of confidence and reduced independence. Exercises that improve balance, leg strength and body awareness can make a real difference. Research summarised by institutions such as the Maryland University of Integrative Health indicates that qigong may help reduce falls in older people.

## Benefits of qigong for seniors

- **Better balance** through slow weight shifting from one leg to the other.
- **Joint mobility** in the shoulders, hips, knees and ankles.
- **Gentle leg strength** from soft knee bends and standing practice.
- **Calmer mind** and often better sleep.
- **Social connection** when practised in a group.

## Why it is safe

- No jumping, running or heavy weights.
- No need to sit on the floor or lie down.
- Every movement can be done seated in a chair.
- The pace is slow and each person moves within their own comfort.

As always, seniors should check with their doctor before starting any new exercise, especially with heart conditions, high blood pressure or recent surgery.

## Three simple practices to try

### Weight shifting

Stand behind a sturdy chair, holding the back lightly. Slowly shift your weight to the right foot, then to the left. Repeat 10 times. This builds balance safely.

### Arm raising

Seated or standing, breathe in as you slowly lift both arms to shoulder height. Breathe out as you lower them. Repeat 8 times. This improves shoulder mobility and breathing.

### Seated breathing

Sit comfortably with feet flat. Place your hands on your belly and take 10 slow, deep breaths. This is a gentle way to calm the mind and lower stress.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is qigong suitable for people over 70?

Yes, with seated options and a slow pace. Check with a doctor first.

### Can someone with knee pain do qigong?

Usually yes, by keeping knee bends very small or practising seated.
