Not everyone lives near a qigong teacher. In India, qigong is still new, and studios are found in only a few cities. The good news is that qigong is very well suited to learning online. The movements are slow, there is no equipment, and you need only a small space at home.

Here is how online qigong classes work and how to get the most out of them.

## Can you really learn qigong online?

Yes. Because qigong is slow and repetitive, it is easy to follow on screen. Live online classes add the benefit of a teacher who can see you and correct your posture. Recorded courses let you practise at your own pace, whenever suits you.

Many students begin online and later attend in-person sessions or healing events when they visit Pune.

## Live classes vs recorded courses

- **Live online classes:** Real-time guidance, corrections, a fixed schedule that builds routine, and a sense of community.
- **Recorded courses:** Practise any time, repeat lessons as often as you like, and learn at your own speed.

Many people combine both: a recorded course for daily practice and a live class once or twice a week for feedback.

## What you need

- A space about two metres by two metres
- A phone, tablet or laptop, ideally on a stand at waist or chest height
- A stable internet connection
- Loose, comfortable clothing
- Bare feet or flat shoes

Set up your device so the teacher can see your full body in live classes.

## How to choose a good online qigong course

- **Check the teacher's background** and how long they have practised.
- **Look for a beginner pathway** that builds step by step instead of random videos.
- **Try a free class first** to see if the teaching style suits you.
- **Ask about support**, such as a way to ask questions between sessions.
- **Look for a practice plan** so you know what to do each day.

## Tips for practising at home

- Practise at the same time each day to build a habit.
- Start with 15 to 20 minutes rather than long sessions.
- Keep a simple notebook of how you feel before and after.
- Join a live session at least once a week to stay motivated.
