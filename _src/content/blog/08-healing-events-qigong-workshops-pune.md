Pune has a thriving wellness scene, from yoga studios in Koregaon Park and Kalyani Nagar to meditation circles across the city. More and more people are also looking for healing events that go beyond a regular class: a longer, deeper experience to reset the body and mind.

If you have seen an Alloha Qi Space healing event and wondered what actually happens there, this guide is for you.

## What is a qigong healing event?

A healing event is a longer, focused session, usually two to four hours or a full day, built around a theme such as stress release, energy renewal or emotional balance. It combines qigong movement, breathwork, guided meditation and quiet reflection in a supportive group setting.

Unlike a weekly class that teaches technique, a healing event is about the experience: slowing down, letting go of tension and leaving with a clear practice to take home.

## What a typical event looks like

- **Welcome and intention:** A short introduction and time to settle in.
- **Opening movement:** Gentle qigong to loosen the body and calm the breath.
- **Main practice:** A themed qigong sequence and guided energy meditation.
- **Rest and integration:** Quiet time, sometimes lying down, to absorb the practice.
- **Sharing and take-home practice:** An optional group reflection and a simple routine to continue at home.

## Who healing events suit

- People feeling burnt out, stuck or low on energy
- Beginners who want a deeper first experience of qigong
- Regular students who want to go further
- Anyone going through a period of change who wants calm and clarity
- Friends, couples or colleagues looking for a meaningful shared experience

No prior experience is needed.

## How to prepare

- Wear loose, comfortable clothes in layers.
- Eat a light meal one to two hours before.
- Bring a water bottle and, if you like, a notebook.
- Arrive 10 to 15 minutes early to settle in.
- Switch your phone to silent.

## A note on expectations

Everyone's experience is different. Some people feel deeply relaxed, some feel energised, and some feel emotional release. All of this is normal. Healing events are a complement to, not a replacement for, medical or psychological care. If you are dealing with a serious health or mental health issue, please continue working with your doctor or therapist.

No. Events are designed so beginners can follow easily.
