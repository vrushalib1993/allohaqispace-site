How you start your morning shapes the rest of your day. Many of us reach for our phones before we even get out of bed, then rush into the day already feeling scattered.

A short qigong routine is a simple way to change that. In just ten minutes, you can wake up your body, steady your breath and set a calm, focused tone for the day. You can do it in your bedroom, on your balcony or on your terrace as the sun comes up.

## Why practise qigong in the morning?

- It loosens joints that feel stiff after sleep.
- It gently raises your energy without the jolt of caffeine.
- It calms the mind before the demands of the day begin.
- It builds a daily habit that is easy to keep.

## Before you begin

Wear comfortable clothes and practise barefoot or in flat shoes. If possible, open a window or step outside for fresh air. Drink a glass of water first.

## The 10-minute morning routine

### 1. Standing and breathing (1 minute)

Stand with feet hip-width apart, knees soft, arms relaxed. Take 6 slow belly breaths and feel your feet on the ground.

### 2. Tapping the body (1 minute)

With loose fists, gently tap down the outside of your arms, across your chest and shoulders, down your legs and up the insides. This wakes up circulation and energy.

### 3. Joint circles (2 minutes)

Make slow circles with your neck, shoulders, wrists, hips, knees and ankles, about 5 circles each. This prepares your body for the day.

### 4. Lifting the sky (2 minutes)

Interlace your fingers in front of your belly. Breathing in, raise your hands overhead, palms up. Breathing out, float your arms down by your sides. Repeat 8 times.

### 5. Drawing the bow (2 minutes)

Step into a wide stance with knees slightly bent. Extend your left arm to the side with the index finger pointing up, while your right hand pulls back as if drawing a bow. Look toward your left hand. Return to centre and switch sides. Repeat 4 times each side. This opens the chest and strengthens the legs.

### 6. Closing (2 minutes)

Stand still with both hands resting on your lower belly. Breathe slowly and notice how your body feels now compared to when you started. Set a simple intention for your day.

## Tips to make it stick

- Practise at the same time every morning, even if only for five minutes.
- Keep your phone in another room until you finish.
- Tick off each day on a calendar to build a streak.
- Pair it with something you already do, like making your morning tea.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I do this routine every day?

Yes. Daily practice gives the best results.

### Should I practise before or after breakfast?

Before breakfast or at least an hour after a meal is best.

### What if I only have five minutes?

Do steps 1, 4 and 6. A short practice every day beats a long one occasionally.
