Many people in Pune search for tai chi classes after seeing those slow, graceful movements in a film or a park video. Then they come across qigong and wonder whether it is the same thing. The two are closely related, but they are not identical, and knowing the difference helps you choose where to begin.

## A quick definition of each

**Qigong** is a broad family of mind-body exercises that combine gentle movement, breathing and focused attention to cultivate energy, or qi. Many qigong forms are short and simple, often a single movement repeated several times.

**Tai chi** (taijiquan) began as a Chinese martial art. It is practised today mostly for health, as a long, continuous sequence of flowing movements called a form. Tai chi is built on qigong principles, which is why people often call it a type of moving qigong.

## The key differences

- **Length and complexity:** A tai chi form can have dozens of linked movements and take months to learn. Qigong routines are usually shorter and quicker to learn.
- **Martial roots:** Tai chi movements come from self-defence techniques. Qigong is mainly focused on health and energy.
- **Repetition:** Qigong often repeats one movement many times. Tai chi flows from one movement to the next without repeating.
- **Learning curve:** Most beginners can follow a qigong routine in the first class. Tai chi takes more memory and patience.

## What they have in common

- Slow, relaxed, flowing movement
- Deep, calm breathing
- Soft knees, upright posture and a relaxed body
- Benefits for balance, flexibility, calm and focus
- Suitable for most ages and fitness levels

## Which one should you start with?

Start with **qigong** if you want quick benefits, a short routine you can do daily, or a gentle practice for stress, stiffness or healthy ageing.

Choose **tai chi** if you enjoy learning a long, beautiful sequence and are drawn to its martial arts history.

Many teachers recommend starting with qigong, because it builds the relaxed posture, breathing and body awareness that make tai chi easier later.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is tai chi a type of qigong?

Tai chi is often described as a form of moving qigong, because it is based on the same principles of breath, energy and relaxed movement.

### Which is better for seniors?

Both are excellent. Qigong is usually easier to start because routines are shorter and can be done seated.

### Can I learn tai chi online?

Yes, though long forms are easier to learn with live correction. Qigong is especially easy to follow online.
