Pune is home to thousands of IT, manufacturing and services companies, and HR teams are under growing pressure to support employee wellbeing. Burnout, long screen hours and hybrid work have made stress and physical discomfort everyday issues in the workplace.

Qigong workshops offer a simple, inclusive and effective wellness activity that almost every employee can join, whatever their age or fitness level.

## Why companies are investing in wellness

Employee wellbeing affects focus, engagement, absenteeism and retention. A good wellness programme shows people they are valued and gives them practical tools to manage stress. The best activities are ones employees actually enjoy and can keep using after the session ends.

## Why qigong suits the workplace

- **No equipment or changing needed:** Employees can join in office clothes.
- **Inclusive:** Suitable for all ages, fitness levels and body types, with seated options.
- **Short and practical:** Even a 10-minute routine can be used at the desk.
- **Calming:** Slow breathing and movement help reduce tension.
- **Works online too:** Ideal for hybrid and distributed teams.

## Workshop formats

- **Lunch-and-learn (45 to 60 minutes):** An introduction to qigong with a simple routine employees can take back to their desks.
- **Stress reset workshop (90 minutes):** Breathing, movement and guided relaxation, focused on burnout prevention.
- **Desk-body workshop (60 minutes):** Practices for back, neck and shoulder stiffness.
- **Weekly programme (4 to 8 weeks):** Regular sessions to build a lasting habit.
- **Offsite and team events:** A calming, shared activity for team retreats.

All formats can run on-site in Pune or live online for teams across India and abroad.

## What employees take away

- A short daily routine they can use at work or home
- Breathing techniques for stressful moments
- Simple stretches for desk-related stiffness
- A calmer, more focused start to the workday

## How to plan a session

- Choose a date and format that fits your calendar.
- Book a meeting room or open space, or share an online link.
- Share a short note with employees about what to expect.
- Collect quick feedback afterwards to shape future sessions.
