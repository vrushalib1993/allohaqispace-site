Welcome to Alloha Qi Space

Alloha Qi Space is a transformative center dedicated to restoring balance, vitality, and well-being through the ancient practices of Zhineng Qigong and Hun Yuan Qi therapy. We believe that true health is not just the absence of disease but a harmonious connection between the body, mind, and energy.

Our mission is deeply personal. Rikhil Bithar and his mother, Ravina Bithar, founded Alloha Qi Space out of genuine care and real experience. Having witnessed firsthand how these practices played a crucial role in healing and recovery within their own family, they felt a strong responsibility to share this wisdom with others. They believe that every family should have access to these powerful tools for healing, prevention, and overall well-being.

At Alloha Qi Space, we provide a nurturing environment for individuals to reconnect with their natural energy, enhance their health, and awaken their inner healing potential.

Our Approach

Our methods are rooted in a profound understanding of Qi (energy), ancient Chinese healing arts, and Ayurvedic principles. Whether you’re looking to heal physical discomfort, reduce stress, or deepen your connection with yourself, our holistic approach provides effective solutions.

We are not just teaching techniques—we are sharing something truly life-changing that has helped us and can help others. Our center exists to spread beauty, wisdom, and well-being, making these practices accessible to everyone.

Our Founder and Expertise

Rikhil Bithar is a certified Hun Yuan Qi Therapist and a Zhineng Qi Gong Level 1 Teacher, specializing in guiding individuals in cultivating and harnessing Qi energy for profound healing and transformation. He has trained under Master Yuantong Liu, learning a structured, science-based approach to Qi cultivation that helps individuals restore balance and vitality.

Rikhil also holds a certification from the Joyful Belly Ayurveda College of Digestion and Nutrition, allowing him to seamlessly integrate Ayurvedic principles into his work. By understanding the bio-characteristics of food, he helps individuals make better dietary choices for long-term health.

In addition, Rikhil has developed the Qi Core Improvement Series—a thoughtfully structured program designed to revitalize the spine and harmonize mental, physical, and energetic systems. Through this comprehensive approach, Rikhil empowers individuals to unlock their full potential, creating a pathway toward balance, coherence, and long-term well-being.

Ravina Bithar, Co-Founder

Ravina Bithar has been a pillar of compassion and wisdom in the journey of Alloha Qi Space. Her deep care for health and well-being, combined with her personal experience of Qi healing, inspired her to co-found this space alongside Rikhil. Together, they have created a place of transformation, healing, and empowerment, ensuring that every individual receives authentic guidance and compassionate support.

Our Mission

Through the science of Zhineng Qigong and Hun Yuan Qi therapy, we guide our students and clients to not only heal physical discomfort but also to create lasting changes in their mental, emotional, and spiritual lives. By harnessing the abundant energy of nature and cultivating a deep connection with one’s inner self, we help individuals overcome challenges, prevent illness, and achieve long-term vitality.

Our Motto – Learn, Own, and Use

We do not just teach methods; we help you embody them in a way that becomes part of your natural way of being. By guiding you to reconnect with your energy and align your practices with the rhythms of nature, we foster a deeper sense of balance and harmony.

Our Vision

At Alloha Qi Space, we believe in the power of nature’s Qi, which surrounds us and is available to anyone who knows how to access it. By teaching the principles of Zhineng Qigong and Hun Yuan Qi therapy, we aim to refine the mental, emotional, and physical aspects of every individual who joins our community. Our vision is not just to heal but to uplift—to help people regain vitality, joy, and purpose in their lives.

We are also dedicated to sharing this knowledge with future generations, creating a ripple effect that promotes health, peace, and well-being for all.
