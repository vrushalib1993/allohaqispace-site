Meet your teacher

Rikhil Bithar teaches every student personally

Rikhil and his mother, Ravina Bithar, founded Alloha Qi Space after seeing first-hand how Qigong supported healing and recovery in their own family. They felt every family should have access to the same simple, practical tools.

Today Rikhil teaches at the Kalyani Nagar centre and live online, shaping each session around your body, your goals and your pace. He also teaches the Qi Core Improvement Series, a six-form system for the spine and overall balance created by his teacher, Master Yuantong Liu.
