Online Healing & Classes

Live online classes, Hun Yuan Qi healing sessions and consultations, wherever you are in the world.

At Alloha Qi Space, we believe that healing and personal growth should be accessible to everyone, no matter where you are in the world. That’s why we’re proud to offer Online Healing and Classes, bringing the transformative power of Zhineng Qi Gong and Hun Yuan Qi Therapy directly to you. Whether you’re in Pune, another part of India, or anywhere across the globe, distance is no longer a barrier to achieving better health and well-being.

How Does Online Healing Work?

You might wonder how energy healing can work online. The truth is, qi—the universal life force energy—knows no boundaries. Just as the sun’s rays reach every corner of the earth, qi can flow effortlessly across distances. Through our online sessions, we connect with you energetically, using the principles of Hun Yuan Qi Therapy to direct healing energy to your body and mind.

Many of our clients have been amazed by their online sessions. Whether you’re dealing with physical pain, emotional stress, or chronic fatigue, the energy we channel during these sessions can help you feel lighter, more balanced, and deeply rejuvenated. It’s a powerful reminder that healing is not limited by physical proximity—it’s about the connection between minds and the flow of energy.

What Our Online Classes Offer

Our online classes are designed to be just as effective as in-person sessions. Through live video sessions, we guide you step-by-step in practices like Zhineng Qi Gong, meditation, and energy healing techniques. These classes are perfect for beginners who are new to Qi Gong, as well as experienced practitioners looking to deepen their practice.

Here’s what you can expect from our online programs:

  • Personalized Guidance: Our instructors provide one-on-one attention, ensuring you understand each movement and technique.
  • Flexibility: Learn at your own pace, from the comfort of your home, at a time that suits your schedule.
  • Community Support: Join a global community of like-minded individuals who are also on their healing journey.

Why Choose Alloha Qi Space for Online Healing?

  • Real experience: Many of our clients have felt meaningful improvements in their well-being through online sessions, from less pain to more energy.
  • Expert Instructors: Our team is trained in Zhineng Qi Gong and Hun Yuan Qi Therapy, with years of experience in both in-person and online healing.
  • Accessible and Affordable: We’ve made our programs affordable and easy to access, so everyone can experience the healing power of Qi.

A Message from Our Founder

“I’ve personally given and received many online healing sessions, and the results have been nothing short of miraculous. The beauty of qi is that it transcends distance, time, and space. Whether we’re in the same room or thousands of miles apart, the healing energy flows just as powerfully. I invite you to experience this for yourself and see how it can transform your life.” – Rikhil B.
